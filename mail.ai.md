# ✉️ mail.ai: AI-Powered Email Management System

## 🎯 Overview
mail.ai is an intelligent email management solution that transforms how organizations and individuals handle their email communications. By leveraging advanced AI, the system automates email processing, categorization, and response management while keeping users informed through their preferred communication channels.
